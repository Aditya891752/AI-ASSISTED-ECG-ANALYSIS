Clinical Problem & Urgency 02 / 10

The Cardiovascular Screening Bottleneck

Millions die annually due to delayed detection of fatal cardiac arrhythmias and silent ischemia.

🚨 The Global Burden
  • #1 Global Killer: Cardiovascular Diseases (CVDs) cause 17.9M deaths/year (32% of all global mortality, WHO).
  • Severe Specialist Shortage: < 1 Cardiologist per 100,000 citizens in Tier-2/3 Indian cities and rural Primary Health Centres (PHCs).
  • The Critical "Golden Hour": Acute Myocardial Infarction (MI) and Ventricular Fibrillation require immediate intervention within 60 minutes to prevent irreversible myocardial necrosis.
⏳ The Holter Diagnostic Dilemma
  • Overwhelming Data Volume: A standard 24-hour Holter monitor logs over 100,000 heartbeats per patient record.
  • Exhausting Manual Triaging: Reviewing a 24-hour trace takes a cardiologist 25–45 minutes of tedious manual scanning.
  • Dangerous Diagnostic Delays: Reports take 48 to 72 hours to reach the patient, risking sudden cardiac arrest in outpatient settings.
💡 The Bioforge Opportunity AI-Accelerated Tele-Cardiology

Transform primary health workers into frontline cardiological responders with instantaneous, automated, and explainable rhythm triaging directly in the browser or on the edge.

Signal Processing Pipeline 05 / 10

End-to-End Signal Processing & Feature Engineering

Bioinformatics pipeline transforming noisy microvolt analog signals into high-confidence predictions.

STEP 1
Filtering & Denoising
  • Butterworth Bandpass: 0.5 Hz – 40 Hz 3rd-order filter removes baseline wander & high-freq EMG.
  • 50/60 Hz Notch: Eliminates powerline interference.
  • Z-Score Normalization: Robust zero-mean unit-variance scaling.
STEP 2
Pan-Tompkins QRS
  • Derivative Filter: Highlights steep QRS slope.
  • Non-Linear Squaring: Amplifies high-frequency energy.
  • Moving-Window Integration: 150 ms envelope detector.
  • Dynamic Dual-Thresholding: Detects R-peaks with > 99% fiducial accuracy.
STEP 3 • INNOVATION
221-D Feature Vector
  • 216-D Morphological: Resampled 180-sample beat window centered on R-peak (-90 to +90 samples).
  • 5-D Dynamic RR Context:
    • Pre-RR & Post-RR intervals
    • Local RR Ratio (Pre / Post)
    • Global RR Ratio (Pre / Mean 10)
    • Differential RR (ΔRR)
STEP 4
Machine Learning
  • Random Forest Ensemble: 100 tuned decision trees with balanced class weighting.
  • Fast Inference: Sub-5ms prediction with tree traversal.
  • Calibrated Probabilities: Per-class posterior probability vector output.
Why RR Context is a Game Changer: Premature beats (like Supraventricular and PVCs) frequently have identical morphology to normal beats but occur prematurely (short pre-RR, long compensatory post-RR pause). Adding the 5-D RR ratios boosted our S-class sensitivity tenfold!

Clinical Safety & Explainability 08 / 10

Doctor-in-the-Loop Triaging & Safety Guardrails

AI serves as an intelligent diagnostic copilot, never an unverified black box.

🔴 HIGH PRIORITY TRIAGE
  • Trigger: Ventricular Ectopic (PVC / VT) OR MI detected with > 80% confidence.
  • Action: Instant visual flash alert, sound chime, automated SMS notification to attending physician.
  • Immediate recommendation for 12-lead confirmatory ECG.
🟡 MODERATE / REVIEW
  • Trigger: Supraventricular Ectopic (S) OR any prediction with confidence < 75%.
  • Action: Flagged in clinical review queue for cardiologist inspection.
  • Displays full 221-D extracted parameters and local RR ratios for validation.
🟢 NORMAL / STABLE
  • Trigger: Normal sinus rhythm [N] with > 90% confidence.
  • Action: Auto-logged into electronic patient chart with routine monitoring schedule.
  • Reduces doctor workload by 80%, filtering out benign baseline rhythms.
🔍 Signal Quality Metric (SQI)
Rejects detached electrodes or motion artifact noise prior to model ingestion, preventing false arrhythmia alarms.
📄 Auditability & Report Generation
Every classified strip includes timestamp, raw lead signals, detected fiducials, and one-click PDF clinical report export.
